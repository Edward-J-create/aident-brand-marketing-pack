# Output Template

The **main document is a usable brand asset library**, not an internal QA report. Follow [reference-pack-blueprint.md](reference-pack-blueprint.md) for category coverage and ordering. Adapt labels to the brand and requested locales, but do not silently remove the visual/video families because no files exist. A focused request may omit unrelated categories only when its narrow scope was explicit.

# {Brand or working brand name} — Marketing Pack

One-line brand descriptor. Under the title show `Source-led` or `Idea-led / working concept`, the date, locales, approval state, and a compact status key: **Original file — ready**, **Original file — review required**, **Reference only**, **Website rendition only**, **Draft copy**, **Concept/brief**, **Missing**, **Blocked**. Call the document a `planning draft` until the ready-file gates pass.

## English

### English Copy

Use a readable two-column table or individual subheadings. Include complete wording for the blueprint's name, tagline, positioning, description lengths, audience blurbs, feature highlights, value propositions, use cases, how-it-works, and integration/availability rows. Where a product feature is not evidenced, write cautious concept wording and label it proposed.

### Official Links and Social Channels

Show actual URL, platform, purpose, and verification state. If not launched, write `Not created` with the required next action; never make up handles or links.

### Brand Assets

Group logo variants/lockups; avatar/profile; social banners; email header; video cover; marketing visuals/ads; product screenshots; and website screenshots. Show a real, reusable item inline at useful quality when supported **and** give its durable original-file location, format, dimensions, rights, and source. If only a website rendition or reference page exists, label it accordingly. For a missing item, include a concise mini-brief: purpose, target size/ratio, source dependency, copy ID, rights/owner, and status. At least one row per required family.

### Launch Video

Embed a real video when supported or show a poster plus a **direct durable link to its inspected original master/export** when supplied and approved. A playlist or watch page belongs under references, not ready files. Otherwise show a labeled concept card with format, duration, 3–5 beats, spoken/on-screen copy, footage source labels, cover, localized/caption variants, and production blocker. Never present a storyboard as a playable asset.

### Video Clips Assets

Inventory or brief logo motion, feature/use-case clips, vertical cuts, and platform-specific variants as relevant. For each real item give the original file, format/duration, poster or player, rights, and direct file location. State what is real, reference-only, planned, and production-blocked.

### The Creative Rule

State the reusable voice, claims, logo/media-use, accessibility, and differentiation rules in a concise operational paragraph or table.

### Platform Template References

Provide actual short-form platform copy patterns and fillable examples for requested channels. Label templates as templates, not published posts. Include the corresponding image/video slot IDs where useful.

### Workflow Ideas

Give 3–5 grounded campaigns or reuse pathways that combine this pack's copy and media slots, with prerequisites and clear next action. Do not turn this into a sprawling marketing strategy.

### Accuracy Guardrails

List disallowed invented claims, source-dependent facts, rights constraints, and approval requirements. In idea-led mode, explicitly say that identity, product behavior, public channels, and media are proposals until confirmed.

## 中文

When Chinese is requested, include **中文 copy** with actual Chinese name, tagline, positioning, description variants, highlights, value, use cases, how it works, and availability/integrations as applicable. Include **中文素材** with Chinese video/subtitle/cover, localized social/marketing visuals, product UI and website screenshots, and content recommendations. Mirror the real/missing/proposed labeling used in English. Avoid an English-only media library with a short Chinese translation tacked on.

## Sources, Decisions, and QA Appendix

Give source URLs and retrieval dates, short evidence notes for consequential claims, original-file library location and verification summary, assumptions, blocked approvals, document version, and a change log. Do not lead with the ledger: the first screen should resemble the reference pack and show assets/copy, not process metadata.
