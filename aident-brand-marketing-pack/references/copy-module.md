# Copy Module

This module produces final text assets. It does not stop at generic suggestions.

## Draft order

1. Write the canonical positioning narrative from the approved brand core.
2. Extract the shortest defensible one-line explanation.
3. Derive requested descriptions, headlines, proof lines, CTAs, and channel variants.
4. Add asset IDs, evidence dependencies, locale, channel, character count when constrained, and approval state.

Do not invent every length independently. Compression must preserve audience, primary value, proof, limitations, and desired action.

## Reusable copy taxonomy

Create only requested items:

- descriptor, one-liner, short description, long description;
- positioning statement, value proposition, message pillars, proof points;
- headlines, subheads, CTA labels, microcopy, metadata, alt text;
- website, email, social, ad, partner, event, creator, sales, or app-store copy;
- visual overlay copy and video hook, voiceover, on-screen copy, captions, and end card;
- localization notes and term locks.

## Channel adaptation

Keep facts stable while adapting structure:

- Website: clear category, value, proof, and next step.
- Social: one idea per post, channel-native opening, concrete proof, explicit CTA.
- Ads: message-match the offer and landing destination; separate primary text, headline, description, and CTA.
- Email: subject and preview must agree with the body; do not hide conditions.
- Creator: use fillable first-person fields unless genuine use or endorsement is verified.
- Partner: define each party's role and approved naming; do not imply an unconfirmed partnership.
- Video: make voiceover and on-screen text complementary; avoid forcing the full script on screen.

## Variant discipline

- Vary one strategic axis at a time: hook, proof, audience frame, objection, or CTA.
- Label the changed axis and intended learning.
- Avoid near-duplicate filler variants.
- Respect platform policy and regulated-claim constraints.
- Never fabricate testimonials, customer names, urgency, scarcity, savings, or performance.

## Length and locale checks

Measure characters after final punctuation and spacing. State the counting convention when the locale makes it ambiguous. Localization preserves meaning, evidence scope, names, and prohibited claims; it is not literal translation.
