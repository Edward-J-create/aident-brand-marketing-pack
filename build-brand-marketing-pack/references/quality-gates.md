# Quality Gates

Use these gates before writing the final editable document and again after reading it back.

## Evidence and accuracy

- Every consequential factual claim maps to a source or is explicitly labeled user-supplied.
- Time-sensitive facts include a retrieval date and are not presented as timeless.
- Conflicting facts remain visible and do not leak into public-ready copy.
- Metrics, testimonials, named customers, prices, security claims, and superlatives have direct support.
- Proposed taglines, concepts, and interpretations are not disguised as existing brand language.

## Message consistency

- Brand, company, product, feature, and integration names use canonical spelling.
- All copy variants inherit the same category, audience, primary value, and proof.
- Short copy is compressed from the approved narrative rather than independently invented.
- Calls to action match the actual next step.
- Creator templates do not imply personal use or endorsement that did not happen.

## Copy checks

- Character-limited variants include measured counts and fall inside the requested range.
- Headlines and captions are readable without missing context.
- Lists avoid repeated claims stated in different words.
- Language is specific to the brand; remove generic filler that could describe any competitor.
- Terminology, punctuation, capitalization, and locale conventions are consistent.
- Localized copy preserves meaning and claim scope rather than translating mechanically.

## Image checks

- Aspect ratio and pixel dimensions match the intended placement.
- Logo and product UI are not fabricated or altered without permission.
- Required brand assets are present and legible.
- Text-safe area works for the intended crop.
- Critical text is not trusted solely to image generation.
- Hands, faces, interfaces, small text, and repeated patterns receive close inspection when present.
- Alt text describes the communication value, not only decorative details.
- The manifest records source, rights, generation model and settings when relevant.

## Video checks

- Duration, aspect ratio, frame rate when specified, and output format match the brief.
- The opening communicates the hook quickly enough for the target channel.
- Product behavior and sequence are accurate.
- On-screen copy is readable at normal playback speed and within safe areas.
- Voiceover, captions, music, and visuals agree.
- Claims stay on screen long enough to read and have evidence.
- No queued, failed, filtered, or partial render is labeled final.
- The delivered file or URL is reachable and the first, middle, and final frames are inspected.

## Document checks

- Title, version, date, language, and pack mode are visible.
- A quick-start summary explains which assets are ready and where they are.
- Headings, tables, links, lists, and media references survived provider conversion.
- The document contains the evidence ledger, gaps, and accuracy guardrails.
- Existing, proposed, generated, ready, deprecated, missing, and blocked assets are distinguishable.
- The created provider document was read back, or the verified Markdown fallback is clearly identified.

## Completion rule

Complete means the requested content exists, the evidence and asset states are visible, the editable document or verified Markdown is accessible, and every claimed final media asset has passed its relevant checks. A polished plan alone is not a finished media deliverable.
