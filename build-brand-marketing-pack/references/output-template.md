# Editable Brand Marketing Pack Template

Use this structure as the portable Markdown master. Remove sections that do not apply; do not leave empty headings or placeholder prose in the delivered document.

## Cover

- Pack title
- Brand and product
- Version
- Date and source retrieval window
- Primary language and markets
- Mode: Library, Production, or Refresh
- Status: Draft, In review, or Approved
- Editable destination

## Quick start

Summarize the brand in one paragraph, then show:

| Verified or available now | Needs review | Missing or blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Approved or verified assets | Proposed copy and media | Evidence, access, rights, or format gaps |

## Channel-ready copy

Create one subsection per requested channel. For each item include asset ID, format, copy, CTA, status, claim IDs, and reuse notes.

Suggested subsections: Website, Product directory, LinkedIn, X or Threads, Instagram or TikTok, YouTube, Email, Partner or creator, and Sales.
