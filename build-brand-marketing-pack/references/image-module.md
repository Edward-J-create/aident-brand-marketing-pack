# Image Module

Use this reference for existing image inventory, image briefs, prompts, or generated images.

## Existing image inventory

Relevant categories include logos for light and dark backgrounds, avatars, channel banners, email headers, product and website screenshots, campaign visuals, diagrams, photos, mockups, video covers, creator assets, icons, QR codes, and partner marks.

For every inventoried file record: asset ID, filename, type, dimensions, background or language variant, source, rights, status, intended placements, and preview or download location. Do not redesign an existing identity while inventorying it.

## Image brief contract

Every requested image needs:

- asset ID, purpose, audience, channel, and placement;
- aspect ratio and exact dimensions when the placement defines them;
- subject, composition, focal point, and text-safe area;
- supplied brand cues and must-use assets;
- copy overlay plan, keeping critical copy editable when practical;
- exclusions and negative constraints;
- accessibility alt text;
- source and rights notes;
- status: proposed, generated, ready, or blocked.

Common deliverables include a product hero, launch visual, feature card, social square or portrait, carousel, blog hero, partner banner, screenshot composition, thumbnail, ad concept, or localized variant. Choose only requested placements.

## Production mode

Before generation, confirm exact count, ratios, resolution, references, rights, and acceptable cost. Generate a small reviewed batch first. Record the model, relevant settings, date, source assets, output link, and QA result.

Treat text rendered inside generated images as unreliable. Verify it closely or render critical typography as an editable layer outside the generated image. Do not invent logos, product UI, customers, endorsements, or people presented as real representatives.
