# Video Module

Use this reference for existing video inventory, concepts, scripts, shot lists, or generated clips.

## Existing video inventory

Relevant categories include launch videos, logo stings, product demos, feature clips, social cutdowns, creator footage, localized voiceover or subtitle versions, covers, thumbnails, and reusable B-roll.

For every inventoried file record: asset ID, filename, purpose, duration, dimensions or aspect ratio, language, source, rights, status, intended placements, and preview or download location.

## Video brief contract

Every requested video needs:

- asset ID, objective, audience, channel, and CTA;
- duration, aspect ratio, and format;
- hook and communication premise;
- beat sheet or timed shot list;
- on-screen text and voiceover when needed;
- product sequence and factual constraints;
- source footage, screenshots, logos, and audio inputs;
- caption, music, and accessibility requirements;
- cover or thumbnail need;
- status: proposed, generated, ready, or blocked.

Common deliverables include a 6–10 second brand sting, 15–30 second teaser, 30–60 second launch video, workflow demonstration, reusable feature clip, creator template, localized version, or vertical cutdown. Choose only requested placements.

## Production mode

Before generation, confirm exact clip count, duration, ratio, model, reference assets, rights, and acceptable cost. Generate or render the smallest proof that can validate the concept before longer or additional variants.

Keep a manifest of model, settings, date, inputs, job status, output link, and QA result. Do not call a queued, partial, failed, or filtered job complete. Inspect the first, middle, and final frames and verify audio, captions, product behavior, and claim readability.
